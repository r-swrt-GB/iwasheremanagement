Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2024

1. Information We Collect

IWasHere collects information that you provide directly to us when using our QR code attendance tracking application. This may include:

  • Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Attendance records and timestamps
  • Event participation data
  • Device information and usage data
  • QR code scan history and location data (when applicable)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the IWasHere attendance tracking service
  • Process and record attendance at events
  • Generate attendance reports and analytics
  • Communicate with you about your account and our services
  • Improve and optimize our application
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With event organizers who have created the events you attend
  • With service providers who assist in operating our application
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • With your explicit consent

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. Attendance records may be retained for historical and reporting purposes unless you request deletion.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your information
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us.

7. Children's Privacy

IWasHere is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us.
